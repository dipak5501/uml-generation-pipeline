# Human evaluation rubric (thesis-aligned, 0–6)

Rate each dimension from 0 (unusable) to 6 (excellent), the same integer scale the VLMs use:

1. Semantic correctness — Does the diagram correctly represent the meaning of the requirements/spec?
2. Structural completeness — Are expected entities, relationships, and structure present?
3. Syntactic accuracy — Is UML/PlantUML notation used correctly and readably?
4. Overall coherence — Is the diagram clear, consistent, and free of contradictions?

Optional free-text comments for qualitative notes.

Do not treat a saved review as the paper's 80-rater study. Quote live Pearson r only after enough reviews exist on this Mac Studio.
